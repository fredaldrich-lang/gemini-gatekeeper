Gemini Gatekeeper

Give your students timed access to the Gemini App.

Domain Activation Checklist

Required Administrative Privileges
To complete activation, you must be a Google Workspace Super Admin or have an executive role with Group Management and Classroom Read privileges.
  1. Provision the Core Access Group: Go to your Admin Console (Directory > Groups) and create a security group named exactly:
    gemini-active-students@yourdomain.com
  2. Configure Gemini Age Restrictions: In the Admin Console, navigate to Generative AI > Gemini app > Service status. Ensure that the service is turned ON for your new group, Then, navigate to Account > Account settings > Age-based access settingand be sure to mark your new group as under 18.
  3. Authorize Data Access: When launching this add-on for the first time as an admin, click "Accept Permissions" on the OAuth consent splash screen to allow the utility to sync roster states.
