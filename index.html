🚀 Setup Wizard Steps

Follow these steps to authorize and link your workspace configurations.

Progress Step 1 of 4

Step 1: Create the Security Group

We require a specific, designated Google Group to route timed student access. This wizard can programmatically deploy it via your active Admin APIs:

Target Group Email Address gemini-active-students@anexampleschool.com

Step 2: Assign Under-18 Safeguards

Google Workspace security rules require that student age parameters be configured manually in your Admin Console.

Action Steps:

1. Open admin.google.com in a new tab.

2. Go to Account settings > Age-based access settings.

3. Search for your newly created Group and ensure it is flagged as "Under 18".

Step 3: Route Core App Access

Now, configure your Google Workspace so that Gemini access is gated exclusively by that group.

Action Steps:

1. Go to Apps > Additional Google Services > Gemini.

2. Select Service Status.

3. Use the left directory filter to select your group, and change its access switch to ON.

Step 4: Secure Production Service Identity

Ensure security by decoupling your master Super Admin account from everyday teacher access triggers.

Least Privilege Strategy: Provision an isolated setup user account. Build a custom administrator role granting it ONLY Group Member Management privileges. Re-deploy this backend App Script executing strictly as that restricted user identity.
